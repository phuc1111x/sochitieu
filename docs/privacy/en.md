# Privacy Policy

**Sổ chi tiêu** · Effective 3 November 2026

### 1. About the app

Sổ chi tiêu ("expense notebook") is a personal expense-tracking app for Android, made by an individual developer. It works offline: your data stays on your phone. The developer runs no server and receives none of your data.

Contact: sanchykids@gmail.com

### 2. Data the app handles

The app only handles what you enter yourself, plus a few of its own settings:

- **Expenses:** amount, category, date, note and the money source used.
- **Money sources:** name, type (cash, bank, e-wallet, credit card…), a credit card's total limit, and the payment app you link to that source.
- **Money in, transfers between sources and balance adjustments.**
- **Settings:** auto-backup, Wi-Fi-only backup, the time of the last backup, whether Google Drive is connected and whether you have seen the swipe hint.

The app does not ask for your name, phone number, account number, card number or banking password. It does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or messages.

### 3. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on your phone, in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. The app does not add its own encryption, so it is best to set a screen lock on your phone. Your data is kept on the phone until you delete it (see section 8).

Your data leaves the phone only in the two cases described in sections 4 and 5.

### 4. Google Drive backup (optional)

- Happens only if you connect Google Drive in the Backup tab and sign in with your Google account.
- When you sign in, Google shares your account's basic profile with the app: email, name and profile picture. The app stores only the email, on your phone, to show it in the Backup tab so you know which account is in use; it is updated after each backup or restore and removed from the phone when you disconnect. The app does not store your name or picture, and does not send your name, email or picture anywhere.
- Besides sign-in, the app requests a single Drive permission: **drive.appdata**. It only allows the app to create, read, update and delete files in a hidden folder reserved for Sổ chi tiêu in your Drive. The app cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.
- The backup is one file, `sochitieu-backup.json`, containing your expenses, money sources, money in, transfers and balance adjustments. Each backup overwrites the previous one; the file is kept until you delete it (see section 8).
- The file goes directly from your phone to Google over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), through no other server.
- Backups run when you tap the button, or automatically when you open the app (at most once every 24 hours) if you turn that on. If you choose "Wi-Fi only", the app checks your phone's network state to know whether it is on Wi-Fi.
- When you restore, the app downloads the backup file and replaces the data on your phone.
- The backup file lives in your Google account and is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.
- Sổ chi tiêu's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

### 5. Android backup

Android may include the app's data in your device backup in your Google account, or copy it to a new phone (for example with Samsung Smart Switch). This is done by Android and Google according to your phone's settings; Sổ chi tiêu does not control it. You can turn it off in your phone's Settings, under Backup or Google.

### 6. Installed payment apps

When you link a money source to a payment app (for example a banking or e-wallet app), Sổ chi tiêu reads the list of apps that have an icon on your home screen so you can choose one. This list is used only on your phone; it is not stored or sent anywhere.

The app stores only the package name of the app you choose, together with that money source (and in your Drive backup if you use it). When you tap "Lưu & mở" (Save & open), Sổ chi tiêu only opens that app. It cannot see or record anything you do in the payment app.

### 7. What the app does not do

- No ads, no analytics or tracking, no automatic crash reports.
- No server of its own. Your data is never sold, shared or transferred to anyone.
- It does not connect to bank accounts and does not make payments.

### 8. How to delete your data

- **One expense:** open the expense → Sửa (Edit) → "Xoá khoản chi này" (Delete this expense).
- **All data on your phone:** uninstall the app, or go to Settings → Apps → Sổ chi tiêu → Storage → Clear data.
- **Revoke Drive access:** in the Backup tab tap "Ngắt kết nối / đổi tài khoản" (Disconnect / switch account), or visit https://myaccount.google.com/connections. Revoking access does not delete the backup file.
- **The Drive backup file:** open https://drive.google.com on a computer → Settings → Manage apps → Sổ chi tiêu → Options → Delete hidden app data.
- **Android backup:** manage it in your phone's Google backup settings.

### 9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not collect data from anyone, including children.
